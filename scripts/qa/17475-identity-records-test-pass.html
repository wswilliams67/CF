Viewport
1440 wide or more. Below that the layout reflows and the column widths in these checks will not match.
Theme
Run once in dark and once in light — the toggle is in the top utility bar.
Nothing here writes. Every screen reads from a demo data seam, so you can click anything without consequence. Refreshing restores the starting state. Two controls are deliberately inert and are called out where they appear.
A

Getting in

The list hands a person to the Identity Records screen. If the wrong person arrives, nothing after this section means anything.

B

Tab 1 — Identities

Every source record attached to this person, as one flat list in payload order. Not grouped by source — the flatness is the point.

C

Tab 2 — Pending matches

People flagged as possibly this person. Read-only: decisions are made in the adjudication queue, and this tab says so.

D

The pair review panel

The evidence behind one pending match. Same content as the queue's own panel, minus the decision bar.

E

Out to the queue and back

The round trip. This is the sequence most likely to strand someone, so walk it exactly as written.

F

Tab 3 — History

One chronological stream, newest first. Two row types share it: ledger decisions and identity facts.

G

Person actions

The two header menus. One of these opens a dialog that is deliberately unfinished — see the note on G4.

H

Edge states

Documented states that look like failures if you meet them cold. Each is reachable from the list.

I

Across the screen

Things that are only wrong when compared with something else, so they survive a section-by-section pass.
