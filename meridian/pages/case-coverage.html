Counting Rules

Custodian workflows — legal hold notification, survey, interview — can run more than once per person, so the cell shows the latest status and a count.

Silent legal holds send nothing. A custodian tracked only under one reads “Silent Legal Hold”, not a gap, and is left out of the not-sent counts.

Data workflows — preservation, collection — are one per source, so the cell rolls up that custodian's sources and the numbers say how many.

Marking a workflow not required requires a reason; it comes out of the gap count and shows in the coverage report as N/A.

A workflow this case does not expect of a custodian shows a dash and is not a gap. Surveys, interviews and collections are expected only where the case says so; notification and preservation always are.
